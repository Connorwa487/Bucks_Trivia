Buck's Night

Connor's Trivia

Shots have been poured. Let's begin.

Setup

Arrange 16 shots in a square — 10 Fireball and 6 Apple Juice. Shuffle so nobody knows which is which.

Connor needs a premix or beer for the whole game.

How to Play

A new reader is chosen each round. They pick a category, draw a question, and read it aloud. The reader cannot answer or steal.

Any player can call "MINE" at any point — even mid-sentence. The reader stops and the player answers without hearing the rest.

Correct — pick any player to take a shot from the table. Connor takes a sip.

Incorrect — that player takes a shot. One other player may call "STEAL" and attempt the full question. Maximum one steal per question.

If nobody calls MINE after the question is read twice, the group may vote to skip it.

Connor cannot be selected to take a shot from the table.

Bonus Questions

Some questions have a bonus, only available to the player who answered correctly.

Nail it — give out an extra shot. Connor takes a shot too.

Miss it — take both shots yourself.

Connor's Drinks

Connor takes a sip every time a question is answered correctly on the first attempt.

Connor takes a shot every time a bonus is answered correctly.

Who's Playing?

Add everyone except Connor.
